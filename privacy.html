Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

At SholoTech ("we", "us", "our"), your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — across our website and the mobile applications we publish (including Image to File Converter, Status Saver, and others).

1. Information we collect

We aim to collect as little data as possible. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Contact details you provide — name and email when you book a call or get in touch.
  • Project details you share with us voluntarily during consultations.
  • Basic usage data — anonymous analytics like pages visited and device type, to improve the site.
  • App permissions — our apps only request permissions needed to function (e.g. storage access for file conversion), and process data on your device wherever possible.
  • In-app activity reward disbursements — when users submit an in-app activity reward disbursement request in SholoTech apps, we collect ONLY their UPI ID solely to send the payout, and strictly no other personal or bank account details.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve our services and apps.
  • To process verified in-app activity reward disbursements solely to the provided UPI ID.
  • To communicate important updates about a project you've engaged us for.

We never sell your personal data or UPI information to third parties.

3. Third-party services

We list only our verified third-party service providers:

  • Unity LevelPlay (for in-app advertising mediation)
  • InsForge (for secure backend database and data storage)
  • Google Analytics and Firebase (for app analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring)
  • Regulated UPI payout partners (used solely to process approved user disbursements)

4. Data retention & security

We keep personal information and payout identifiers only as long as needed for the purposes described here, and delete or anonymize them securely thereafter. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

6. Children's privacy

Our services and mobile applications are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
