Frequently Asked Questions

Does OTP Saver read all my personal messages?
No. The app strictly filters incoming messages for standard verification code patterns (e.g., OTP, verification code, 2FA) and ignores personal conversations.
Does the app require internet access?
No. The app operates completely offline without needing an active internet connection.
Can someone else see my stored codes if they borrow my phone?
You can enable Biometric Fingerprint or PIN lock inside OTP Saver so that nobody can open the app without your authorization.
Is OTP Saver free?
Yes, OTP Saver is 100% free to download and use on Android through the Google Play Store.
