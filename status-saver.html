Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download a status using Status Saver?
View the status first in your WhatsApp or WA Business app. Then open Status Saver — the viewed photos and videos will appear automatically. Tap the download icon to save them to your device gallery.
Does the sender know that I downloaded their status?
No. Status Saver reads locally cached files on your phone. The original poster is never notified when you save or view statuses inside Status Saver.
Is this app free to use?
Yes! Status Saver - Save & Repost is 100% free to download and use on Android.
Where are the saved statuses stored?
Saved statuses are immediately accessible in your phone's default Gallery app and in the built-in Saved Gallery tab inside the app.
