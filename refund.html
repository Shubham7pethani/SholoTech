Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy details the policies and terms governing custom software and mobile application development services, digital purchases, and in-app activity reward disbursements provided by SholoTech ("we", "us", "our").

Custom software and mobile application development fees are governed by signed project agreements.

Digital software products, in-app features, and processed in-app activity reward disbursements are final and non-refundable once delivered or processed.

Once a verified reward disbursement request has been processed and disbursed through our regulated UPI payout partners to the user-submitted UPI ID, the disbursement is strictly final, non-reversible, and non-refundable.
